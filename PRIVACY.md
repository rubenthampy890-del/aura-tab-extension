# Privacy Policy for Aura Tab

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Aura Tab is a browser new-tab extension. It does not use a developer-operated analytics service, advertising service, or account system, and the developer does not sell user data.

## Data stored by the extension

Aura Tab stores settings, layouts, notes, tasks, quick links, timer state, saved tab groups, clipboard snippets that you choose to capture, and wallpaper files in browser-managed storage. Small preferences may be stored in Chrome Sync when that feature is available, which means Google may synchronize those preferences through the signed-in Chrome account. Wallpaper files and other larger items remain in local browser storage.

## Optional browser access

Aura Tab requests access to tabs, most-visited sites, and extension management only when the corresponding feature is enabled. That information is used to render the requested dashboard tool. Aura Tab does not send it to the developer.

## External network requests

Some features contact third-party services directly from the browser:

- Open-Meteo receives weather coordinates. The current release uses a fallback location when browser location access is unavailable.
- Pinterest and its image CDN are contacted only when the Pinterest wallpaper tool is used.
- Google Fonts and preset wallpaper providers may receive ordinary web-request information when their resources are loaded.
- Custom links, wallpaper URLs, and embedded widgets contact the destinations selected by the user.

Those services have their own privacy policies. Aura Tab does not add tracking identifiers to these requests.

## Clipboard data

Clipboard capture is opt-in and operates only while interacting with the Aura Tab page. Captured snippets are stored in local browser storage and are not sent to the developer.

## Deletion and retention

Use **Reset to Defaults** in Aura Tab 1.1.1 or later to clear settings, notes, shortcuts, timer data, clipboard history, and locally stored wallpaper files. Uninstalling the extension also removes its browser-managed data, subject to the browser provider's sync and retention behavior.
